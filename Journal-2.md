## Limits and next work

The command validates all tiers and indexes photo, video, and LiDAR inputs; RGB pairing remains unresolved. The photo smoke fixture used distinct valid PNG files to exercise decoding and source references, not real camera photos or geometry inference. The standalone MP4 test came from the supplied scan and had no sidecars. Property-plan schema validation, semantic checks, plan rendering, and common-contract output remain open. Continue with T21–T23 and keep T02 open until property-plan output and evaluation reuse stable physical IDs.
