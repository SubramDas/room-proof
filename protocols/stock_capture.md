# RoomProof stock capture card — Route 2, draft 1

Use an iPhone 15 or newer for **Camera photos and ordinary Camera video**. For **LiDAR**, use an iPhone with a LiDAR Scanner, iOS 18.6 or later, and [Stray Scanner 1.4](https://apps.apple.com/in/app/stray-scanner/id1557051662). The tested owner phone is an iPhone 15 Pro Max. These are three **separate captures of the same spaces**. Keep all originals. Record your start, stop, export, and transfer times.

1. **Before capture:** Charge the phone and check available space in Settings → General → iPhone Storage; the full-home storage need has not yet been measured. Install Stray Scanner only for LiDAR capture on a phone that has LiDAR; allow Camera and Motion access if prompted. Photo and video use the built-in Camera app only. Assign one property ID (`prop-home`) and a stable ID to every room, hall/connector, and stair/landing (`room-living-room`, `room-hall`). Keep those IDs with the human-readable room labels and reuse them for all tiers. Do not enter tape/laser dimensions into a capture app.
2. **Photos:** Open **Camera → Photo**, rear **1×** camera; use ordinary stills (no Portrait, panorama, or screenshot). Create one folder per space afterward; name each folder with its stable `room-...` ID. Take **2–8 photos per space**. Together they must show every wall and corner, floor–wall and wall–ceiling edges, the ceiling, doors/windows, and each visible damage patch. Stand back enough to include full walls; add a closer damage view only after coverage. Photograph every shared doorway from **both** sides. Include the hall as its own folder. Check that no space has fewer than two or more than eight images.
3. **Video:** Open **Camera → Video**, rear **1×** camera, choose **1080p HD / 30 fps** at the top of the screen, and use ordinary Video mode (not Cinematic, Action, or time-lapse). Record **one continuous clip**. Start in the labelled first room, pan slowly across all walls, floor/ceiling edges, openings, and damage; walk slowly through each doorway and connector to cover every space. Face both sides of shared doors and revisit one known area before stopping. Do not splice or trim the clip.
4. **LiDAR:** Open **Stray Scanner** and use its default recording settings; note the displayed frame rate if shown. Tap the video capture button to start before entering the first room. Hold the phone steadily, sweep the floor edge, each wall, ceiling edge, openings, and damage, then walk through every connector and room. Revisit a starting doorway or wall; tap the capture button to stop and tap **Share**. Sharing creates a **ZIP**. Save or transfer that ZIP unchanged; on the computer, extract a copy for processing. The extracted folder should contain `rgb.mp4`, `camera_matrix.csv`, `odometry.csv`, `imu.csv`, `depth/`, and `confidence/`; optional `distortion/` is fine. Do not rename or edit files inside it.
5. **Review and hand off:** Re-shoot a space if a wall/opening is missed, images blur, a doorway has only one side, the phone reports tracking loss, or dark/mirror/glass areas hide coverage. Note difficult surfaces even after recapture. Transfer **original** stills, the standalone video, and the original Stray ZIP. Keep photo folders labelled; place the video separately. Check the transfer contains every folder and the full scan; send the property/room labels, device model, iOS/app versions, settings, any recaptures, and the four timing values. Keep tape/laser truth in a separate sheet, never inside the capture folders.

The owner reports an unfamiliar person's trial finished in under five minutes; a timing breakdown and repeat observation were not recorded. Keep the complete ZIP even if a later format check reports a missing frame.
