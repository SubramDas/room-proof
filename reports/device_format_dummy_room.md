**Format mismatch:** There is **one fewer RGB frame than pose/depth/confidence frames**. The same one-frame difference appears in the three older starter scans (1,714 vs 1,715; 5,250 vs 5,251; 9,744 vs 9,745), so code must not assume equal counts. The missing RGB frame’s position is not established. The importer should preserve all raw files, flag the mismatch, and avoid silently pairing the extra depth/pose sample with an arbitrary RGB frame. A fresh scan should be checked the same way.

**Still unverified:** camera-to-depth calibration beyond the published format; camera-to-world versus world-to-camera transform direction and axis convention; per-frame RGB timestamps and exact offset; room dimensions, openings, drift, and depth error against tape/laser truth. The owner scan does not provide a matched standalone Camera video or still-photo tier. The first pass establishes file format and transfer integrity only.
